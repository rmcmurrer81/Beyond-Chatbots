## Part 1 — Set up the question (slides 1–7, 0:00–8:15)

**Slide 1 — Beyond Chatbots (0:00–0:45):** "Good afternoon. I'm the founder of Kira Labs. Today I want to show how we can test whether an AI remembers correctly, keeps identities separate, and reports what actually happened."

**Slide 2 — Continuity (0:45–2:00):** "Imagine Kira sees a blue cup. Lisa never entered the room. Lisa may learn about the cup from Kira, but must not remember personally seeing it."

**Slide 3 — Model and retained state (2:00–3:15):** "A language model, temporary chat context, and saved memories are different things. We need to know what survives a restart and who owns it."

**Slide 4 — Kira World (3:15–4:30):** "Kira World is the planned home for distinct synthetic identities in digital spaces. They can share software, but their personal histories should remain separate."

**Slide 5 — Identity isolation (4:30–5:45):** "Someone may receive another person's report without acquiring that person's first-hand experience."

**Slide 6 — Corrections (5:45–7:00):** "If we discover the cup was green, the current answer should change, and the correction should have a source. Privacy and deletion rules still matter."

**Slide 7 — Origins (7:00–8:15):** "An observation, report, guess, and dream are not the same kind of evidence. A trustworthy memory system keeps them separate."

## Part 2 — Show the results (slides 8–14, 8:15–18:50)

**Slide 8 — Held and failed work (8:15–9:30):** "Saving a video request is not the same as finishing a video. Systems must distinguish requested, held, running, finished, and uncertain work."

**Slide 9 — Receipts (9:30–10:45):** "If a task finishes but confirmation is lost, blindly retrying can duplicate it. We need an operation ID and a verifiable receipt."

**Slide 10 — Test protocol (10:45–12:00):** "Don't judge an AI by one impressive example. Compare fair tasks, strong simple controls, and recorded failures."

**Slide 11 — Five-seed review retention (12:00–14:00):** "Our small command learner retained all old and new examples with review across five repeated trials. That's a narrow finding, not proof of general conversation."

**Slide 12 — Visual failure (14:00–15:40):** "This experiment failed: the learner memorized training images but scored zero on new color-and-shape combinations. Failures tell us what needs work."

**Slide 13 — Resource measurements (15:40–17:30):** "This tiny command model uses little memory for its task, but it has not been shown to outperform a full Qwen conversation model."

**Slide 14 — October 10 update (17:30–18:50):** "Just this week, four delayed NewBrain restarts passed independent verification. All 10,720 history records were preserved, and all 64 comparison pairs matched in each test. All four planned restart tests are now complete. The saved static body rig reopens correctly; real movement and reliable general memory are unfinished."
