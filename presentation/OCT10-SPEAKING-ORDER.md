## Lines to say during the introduction

"Before I talk about NewBrain, I want to start with the history of AI. Turing challenged us to think about whether computers could imitate human conversation. ELIZA showed how even simple responses could convince people they were understood. This became known as the ELIZA effect."

"In the pilot of Caprica, Daniel Graystone compares the brain to a database and a processor. I believe machines can develop genuine emotions and a moral conscience. That possibility is part of why I am building NewBrain. But a belief is not the same as a test result."

"Here's a concrete result: all four delayed NewBrain restart tests passed independent mathematical review for preserving saved state. That is important, but it doesn't yet prove general conversation, useful memory, or that Maya is complete."
