LANGUAGE REVIEW RETENTION — PASSED WITH NARROW SCOPE
The small command learner has 25 vocabulary entries and 4,619 trainable parameters. Across five fresh seeds on the same already exposed task, every review arm answered 216/216 old and 162/162 new examples correctly. Without review, old-task scores ranged from 78/216 to 105/216; the pooled score was 458/1,080 (42.41%). One no-review arm scored 49/54 on new wording. Both forks used 400 updates with batch 24, but review used 7,200 new+2,400 old exposures and no-review used 9,600 new exposures. A handwritten grammar also scored 100%. This establishes repeatability on this toy task, not general conversation or human learning. Fifteen replication reloads were in memory; no new replication checkpoints were saved to disk.

VISUAL LEARNING — GENERALIZATION FAILED
One seed learned 96/96 training images. It scored 27/72 (37.5%) with changed position, size and brightness,0/48 on new color/shape combinations, and 0/36 when both changes occurred. Color stayed correct; shape generalization failed. A pixel-rule control answered 252/252. At confidence 0.70 the learner still accepted 67 wrong withheld answers. The weights were process-local and not saved. No integrated perception or hearing result follows.

NEWBRAIN MIGRATIONS — FOUR OF FOUR INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED
Each saved mathematical review covered 64 comparison pairs,256 generation steps,six out-of-vocabulary pairs and all 10,720 history rows, retaining owners, parents and zero added training updates. Experimental 12,256-history work remains unadopted. This establishes tested migration equivalence and preservation, not useful new learning, reliable general memory or conversation.

DELAYED NEWBRAIN RESTARTS — ONE OF FOUR INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED
The first delayed NewBrain restart passed independent saved-output mathematical review. All 10,720 original history records were preserved; all 64 comparison pairs, 256 generation steps and six out-of-vocabulary pairs were checked with zero added training updates. Both genuine delay clocks exceeded 44 minutes. One of four delayed restart results is accepted; three restart tests remain. The review used saved outputs and did not rerun the model. Earlier path-spelling, read-accounting and auditor failures remain retained. This establishes preservation for this restart, not useful new learning, reliable general memory or conversation.
