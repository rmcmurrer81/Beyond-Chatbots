# Reuse policy — current restrictions

This page preserves the current release boundary. It does **not** select a new code, model, voice, artwork or data license.

The [project overview](../README.md) identifies Robert McMurrer / Kira Labs and states that downloading and viewing the presentation materials is intended. Keep the attribution and source/scope notices with those materials. Conceptual artwork is illustrative, not anatomical or experimental evidence.

No general software or artwork license is granted by the current documents. Public visibility and Download ZIP do not by themselves settle permission to redistribute code/assets, create derivatives, use them commercially or reuse a voice/model. Do not describe this repository as generally open-source on that basis.

The original NewBrain and KiraWorld repositories remain private. Future public copies have a separate exact-file release review; publishing a generic subset does not authorize private identities, histories, learned state, appearance assets or other omitted material. Code and assets may also carry separate third-party notices.

Until Robert chooses and publishes a license or an explicit permission statement for the relevant files:

- Use the current materials for their stated downloading/viewing purpose.
- Preserve attribution, evidence limitations and any retained third-party notices.
- Seek specific permission for reuse beyond the stated purpose through the existing contact in the [project overview](../README.md).
- Do not infer voice, likeness, model-weight or reference-data rights from a source-code release.
- Do not upload private state or unapproved code to make a pending demo appear complete.

An eventual license/permission decision must identify the covered files and exclusions. This page should then be updated to link that actual decision. It grants no rights on behalf of unverified third-party owners.
