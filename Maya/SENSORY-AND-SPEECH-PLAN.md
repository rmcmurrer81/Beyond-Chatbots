# Proposed pre-birth checks

Use independently licensed held-out image, audio and video fixtures with controlled baselines and no answer leakage. Record actual adapter provenance, input modality, timestamp/clock, output, correction and restart retention.

Images: test object/relationship/uncertainty questions against no-image and shuffled-image controls.
Audio: test hearing from waveform against transcript-only, muted and mismatched-audio controls. Transcript access is not hearing.
Video: test ordered events, synchronization and correction against shuffled frames, static-image and mismatched audio/video controls.
Words: test question-local interpretation and memory admission without promoting unsupported assertions.
Connected perception: distinguish the character's actual adapter path from host assistance or human summaries.
Speech: preserve a genuine audio recording and aligned mouth/lip/tongue observations. Score intelligibility, timing and correction; a text response or moving mesh alone is not audible talking.

Preserve adverse results, false memories, corrections, cold/restart retention and latency. No source-only plan waives birth/stage/body criteria. No such test ran during packaging.
